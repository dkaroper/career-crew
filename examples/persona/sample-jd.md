Laurentide Composites (fictional company used as an example)
Quality Technician / Technicienne ou technicien qualité
Montréal, QC (on-site, day shift)

About us

Laurentide Composites makes carbon-fibre and glass-fibre structural parts for regional
aircraft, rail interiors, and medical imaging tables. Our 120-person plant runs
autoclave and out-of-autoclave lines plus a five-axis machining cell for trimming and
drilling cured parts. We are growing our quality team ahead of a new rail interiors
contract.

What you will do

- Perform incoming, in-process, and final inspection of cured and machined composite
  parts against drawings, specifications, and customer requirements.
- Program and run our CMM for first-article and production inspection; maintain
  inspection programs as drawings change.
- Write nonconformance reports, contain suspect product, and lead root-cause and
  corrective-action investigations (8D) with production and engineering.
- Maintain the gauge calibration program and records.
- Collect and chart SPC data on critical dimensions and flag trends to the production
  supervisor.
- Support internal and customer audits under our AS9100 quality system.
- Take part in continuous improvement projects that reduce scrap and rework.

What you bring

- DEC or AEC in mechanical engineering technology, manufacturing, or a related field.
- 2 to 5 years of inspection experience in a manufacturing environment, ideally
  aerospace or precision machining.
- Hands-on CMM experience (PC-DMIS an asset) and confident use of hand gauges.
- Working knowledge of GD&T, SPC, and structured problem solving (8D, 5 Whys).
- Experience with ISO 9001 or AS9100.
- Bilingual French and English, spoken and written; our customers work in both.
- Assets: composites inspection (visual, tap test, ultrasonic NDT), ASQ CQT
  certification, SAP or another ERP.

What we offer

- Salary range 58,000 to 68,000 CAD, depending on experience.
- Group insurance and RRSP matching from day one.
- Paid training, including NDT Level 1 for the right candidate.

To apply, submit your resume through our careers page. We thank all applicants; only
those selected for an interview will be contacted.
