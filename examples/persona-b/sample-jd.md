Harbourview Family Health (fictional clinic used as an example)
Medical Office Administrator
Halifax, NS (on-site, Monday to Friday)

About us

Harbourview Family Health is a collaborative family practice with six physicians, two
nurse practitioners, and a dietitian serving about 9,000 patients in Halifax's north
end. We are adding a third administrator as we take on patients from the provincial
waitlist.

What you will do

- Greet patients, check them in, and keep the waiting room running on time.
- Book, confirm, and reschedule appointments across several providers' calendars.
- Update and maintain patient records in our EMR, including scanning and indexing
  referrals, lab results, and specialist letters.
- Submit physician billing to the provincial plan, reconcile remittances, and correct
  rejected claims.
- Answer a busy phone line and triage requests to the right provider or team member.
- Order medical and office supplies and keep inventory records.
- Protect patient privacy under provincial health-information law.

What you bring

- A diploma in medical office administration or equivalent experience.
- 2+ years in a medical office, clinic, or hospital administrative role.
- Hands-on EMR experience (Accuro or Med Access an asset).
- Knowledge of medical terminology and physician billing.
- Calm, friendly communication with patients, including in difficult conversations.
- Strong organization and accuracy in a fast-paced setting.
- Assets: French, current First Aid and CPR, experience with a waitlist intake.

What we offer

- 23 to 26 dollars an hour, depending on experience.
- Health and dental benefits after three months.
- Weekdays only; no evenings or weekends.

Apply with your resume through our website. We thank all applicants; only those
selected for an interview will be contacted.
